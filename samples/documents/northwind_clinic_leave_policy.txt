Northwind Clinic: Staff Leave Policy (fictional sample document)

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Unused annual leave can be carried over for a maximum of three months into the next year; after that it expires.

Requesting leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the staff portal at least 14 days in advance. Requests during the December holiday period are approved on a first-come, first-served basis, and no more than two nurses from the same ward may be on leave at the same time.

Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required after two consecutive days of absence. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

Maternity and paternity leave
Mothers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid maternity leave. Fathers are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within three months of the birth.

Contact
Questions about this policy go to the HR office on the ground floor, extension 214.
